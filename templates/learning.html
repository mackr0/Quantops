Learning Scoreboard

Is the system learning? Each arm (one model, three replicate profiles) is scored per ISO week on its own resolved decisions: directional hit rate, calibration (hit rate at stated confidence ≥ {{ b.high_conf }} vs below, and the Brier score — lower is better, 0.25 is a coin at 50% stated), HOLD quality (inside ±{{ b.hold_band }}%), and weekly equity return against SPY with the replicate spread. "Learning" is these lines sloping the right way over weeks; "better model" is one arm above the others across all of its replicates. This page issues no verdict — the pre-registered decision rule in the plan does that at the horizon. Every rate shows its sample size; weeks under {{ b.thin_week_n }} directional decisions are marked thin.
